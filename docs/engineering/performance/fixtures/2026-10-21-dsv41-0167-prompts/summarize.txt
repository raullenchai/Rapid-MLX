Transcript: Marlow Museum evening-opening pilot
Date: February 4, 2027, 10:00-10:45
Participants: Elena Park, director; Sam Ortiz, operations; Priya Shah, visitor services; Theo Bell, finance; Noor Ellis, education; June Reed, communications.

Elena: We need a practical decision on Thursday evenings. The proposal is to stay open until 9 p.m. for six weeks, from March 11 through April 15. Today we must settle staffing, ticket rules, a budget ceiling, and how we will judge the pilot.

Priya: We surveyed 186 visitors last month. Ninety-two said they would consider an evening visit, but only 41 specifically preferred Thursday. That is a signal of interest, not an attendance forecast. My team suggests a target of 120 visitors per evening.

Theo: The draft adds $4,800 in wages, $900 in security costs, and $600 for printing and local promotion. That totals $6,300 for the entire pilot. I can reserve that amount, but spending above it needs Elena's written approval.

Sam: We can operate the ground floor and the east gallery with two visitor assistants and one security officer. Opening the upstairs collection would require a second officer because of the stairwell sight line. I recommend keeping upstairs closed and placing signs at the entrance before people buy a ticket.

Noor: Closing upstairs works for education. I propose a twenty-minute object talk at 6:30 and another at 7:30, with a maximum of 25 people per talk. I will prepare a short script and accessibility notes, but I cannot personally deliver all twelve sessions.

Priya: Two trained volunteers can alternate talks. I can ask for availability by February 12, then publish a final rota by February 19.

Elena: Agreed. Priya owns the rota, and Noor owns training and the script. Let's put the training on February 25 and require the script by February 18 so volunteers have a week to read it.

June: Ticketing needs a simple story. I suggest the usual adult price of $8, free admission for under-18s, and existing membership benefits. The website must clearly say that the upstairs collection is closed and that talks have limited capacity.

Theo: Keep normal prices. For reporting, I need paid tickets, free tickets, and member visits counted separately. We should also record incremental cost per opening, including actual staff hours, so unused budget does not look like unexplained savings.

Sam: One complication: the east entrance ramp inspection is scheduled for March 9. It should pass, but we cannot promise. I will seek an earlier inspection by February 10 and report its status by February 24. If approval is still outstanding on March 5, Elena should decide whether to delay the launch.

June: I need the approved details by February 22 to send posters to print. I can update the website on February 26 and schedule social posts for March 1. Can we approve digital promotion first and release printing after Sam's report?

Elena: Yes. June can prepare both, but printing is conditional on the February 24 access update. The $600 promotion allocation remains a ceiling. Sam, send June the confirmed route description as part of that update.

Noor: We also need feedback beyond attendance. A two-question exit card could ask whether evening hours enabled the visit and whether the visitor would return on a Thursday. No names or email addresses. I will draft it by February 16, and Priya can have the desk offer one card per visiting group. Cards should be optional.

Theo: I will build a shared reporting sheet by February 15. Priya will enter attendance and card totals the next morning; Sam will add paid hours and security charges each Friday. I will calculate the final cost and revenue comparison by April 22.

Elena: Decision confirmed: six Thursdays, ground floor and east gallery only, normal prices, and a $6,300 maximum. Success means an average of at least 120 visitors per opening and at least 60 percent of returned cards saying evening hours enabled the visit. Theo will convene the review on April 26. We will decide then whether to extend, change, or end the arrangement.
