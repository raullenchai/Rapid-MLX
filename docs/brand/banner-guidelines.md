# Rapid-MLX banner guidelines

This is the durable implementation summary for Rapid-MLX marketing banners.
It applies to README heroes, release art, social cards, blog headers, animated
loops, and mascot stickers.

## Brand assets

- Always composite the approved cheetah PNG. Never regenerate, redraw,
  recolor, outline, crop, distort, or add a shadow or props to the mascot.
- Preserve the approved cheetah's original orientation and motion streaks,
  scale it uniformly, and keep clear space of at least half its head width.
- Place the mascot only on cream, white, or the approved dark panel. Never put
  it on a photo or busy gradient.

## Color

- Paper: `#F9F7F3`
- Ink: `#141414`
- Cheetah orange: `#EFA23A`
- Pill background / text: `#EEF2F7` / `#3A5C86`
- Hairlines: `#E2DFD6`
- Dark panel: `#17171A` with white text
- Use no more than one saturated color per banner. Blue belongs only in pills
  and links. Subline text is ink at 50% opacity.

## Typography and copy

- Use Cera Pro Medium and Bold. Poppins is the only approved fallback.
- Commands, aliases, and small technical labels use JetBrains Mono or SF Mono.
- Use sentence case, with the claim as the largest element. Do not use emoji,
  exclamation marks, italics, all-caps headlines, or generic marketing hype.
- Copy must be plain, specific, and measured. Prefer a verified number to an
  adjective, pair every speed number with its hardware, and refresh claims
  against current measurements before publishing.

## Composition

- Square release: `1800×1800`, centered stack, optional release pill, original
  plate, mascot at 25–35% canvas width, one claim of at most 20 characters,
  one-sentence subline, and exactly three two-word chips. Keep the bottom 12%
  clear for the approved Rapid-MLX lockup.
- Social card: `1600×900`, 6% margins, mascot on the left and left-aligned copy
  on the right.
- Blog or README header: `2400×1000`, claim only—no chips or release pill.
- Sticker/avatar: `1024×1024`, mascot on cream with 12% padding and no text.
- Animated loop: `1080×1080`; only mascot bob/tilt, trailing streaks, and two
  or three dust puffs may move. No camera motion, zoom, parallax, flashing, or
  color shift.

## Never use

Gradients, glow, neon, 3D rendering, photo backgrounds, extra characters,
fake logos, lens flare, mascot drop shadows, distorted text, or watermarks.
